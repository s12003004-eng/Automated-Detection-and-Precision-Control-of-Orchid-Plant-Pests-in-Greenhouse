## 📌 PROJECT OVERVIEW

Orchid cultivation is a high-value sector in Sri Lanka's ornamental plant industry, serving both domestic and export markets. However, yield and plant quality are frequently compromised by pests such as mealybugs, aphids, scale insects and thrips. Early detection is critical, but manual inspection in small greenhouses is labor-intensive, time-consuming and prone to human error, especially when small pests hide under leaves or along stems.

This project proposes the development of an autonomous robot designed to inspect orchid plants and control pests within a small-scale greenhouse. The robot utilizes an infrared (IR) line-following system for navigation and ultrasonic sensors for obstacle detection. A custom PCB will be developed to integrate the sensors, motors and actuators. A Raspberry Pi processes close-up images captured by an onboard camera to identify pests. Subsequently, the robot automatically determines the appropriate course of action, which may involve mechanical removal and the spraying of a targeted liquid. A robotic arm positions the necessary tool or nozzle at the specific location requiring treatment. Results can be monitored via a dashboard.
